Frequently Asked Questions

How current is the table availability shown in the app?

Employees update table status from an in-house tablet, and the app checks for updates automatically. It can take a few minutes for a change to appear — pull down on the Tables tab to check right away.

Why doesn't Fuquay-Varina have a menu in the app yet?

Break Time is in the process of taking over that location. The menu will be added once that transition is complete — everything else (table availability, hours, location) is live in the meantime.

How do I add a tournament to my calendar?

Tap any event on the Tournaments & Events tab and confirm the calendar prompt. The app only ever creates that one event — it can't read anything already on your calendar.

Is my location or personal data shared?

No. The app doesn't collect personal information, doesn't have accounts, and doesn't request access to your location, contacts, camera, or photos. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

I found incorrect info (hours, beer list, event details) — how do I report it?

Email us at chasessoftware@gmail.com with what's wrong, and we'll get it corrected.
